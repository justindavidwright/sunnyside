★ Notable finds Several species here are genuinely exciting for the area. The Wood Thrush, Veery, Worm-eating Warbler, and Ovenbird are all declining neotropical migrants that prefer mature forest interior — their presence suggests the property sits within quality habitat. The May 5th recording session was exceptional, likely coinciding with a peak migration pulse through the river valley.
